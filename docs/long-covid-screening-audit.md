# Long COVID screening audit — 2026-09-06

Scope: the locally exported website data and its generation records. The upstream Long COVID project is undergoing an update; its current working files must not be mixed into the published snapshot. This audit does not independently validate clinical eligibility or individual extraction results.

## Findings and corrections

1. **The 4,125 “not retrieved” count is unsupported.** The old generator computed `21,790 relevance-positive rows - 17,665 screening rows`, without joining DOI sets. The screening rows include 1,439 relevance-negative, 838 unknown and 8 parse-error records. These are not nested populations. The legacy `screen_for_trials.py:read_abstract_text` reads abstracts first and at most the first three PDF pages as fallback. Thus screening rows are not evidence of full-text retrieval. The old full-text and retrieval labels have been removed.
2. **There is a traceable historical retrieval-failure list.** `OLD_CODE_ARCHIVE/pull_long_covid_papers/missing_pdfs_report.html` explicitly describes 337 reports whose PDFs/MDs could not be obtained automatically, dated 2026-03-08. Its 337 distinct DOIs and source SHA-256 are preserved in `data/birds_eye_reviews/long_covid/retrieval_failures_2026-03-08.json`. Two of those DOIs now occur in the exported extractions; 335 do not. These 335 are not proven current retrieval failures: later retrieval, selection or incomplete extraction could explain their absence. The new box labels the historical count and this limitation. It must not be deducted again from another stage. This list also includes reports later marked excluded (9 in the exported screening CSV), so it is not a list of 337 confirmed eligible trials.
3. **“Not excluded” was incorrectly called “eligible.”** Of 17,665 screening rows, 11,815 are flagged excluded, 4,662 explicitly not excluded, and 1,188 have blank exclusion flags. The old 5,850 eligible total included all 1,188 blanks. Even an explicit non-exclusion is not confirmation of eligibility: 2,529 of the 4,662 explicitly non-excluded rows say they do not study treatment. The corrected label is “not flagged excluded,” with unresolved flags stated separately.
4. **The extraction flow was not a linked cohort.** The old 1,922 extractions counted two upstream feeds, while the exported feed currently contains 786 rows. The 5,850-to-1,922 drop was unexplained; raw feed sizes can also include duplicates or records excluded in screening. The corrected flow joins screening DOIs to exported extraction IDs, stripping arm suffixes just as the dashboard does. Currently 775 non-excluded screening reports have exported extractions and 5,075 do not. Absence of an extraction is not called a scientific exclusion or failed retrieval.
5. **The saved dashboard endpoint is stale.** `prisma.json` says 989 (289 RCT / 700 observational); the exported data and current dashboard server predicate yield 775 extraction rows (282 flagged randomized / 493 other). Of 786 exported rows, 11 are excluded in screening and zero additional rows fail the design-presence/non-clinical-label predicate. Counts now come from the same exported files on page generation. The existing upstream generator also omits the dashboard's screening-exclusion predicate, and references `Path` without importing it; it should not be used to restore the old funnel.
6. **Extraction rows, reports and studies were conflated.** DOI deduplication does not establish independent studies; multiple reports can concern the same trial, and arm suffixes can create multiple extraction rows per report. The new accounting labels these units explicitly. The terminal count precedes the dashboard's `preferPublished` rule and interactive filters, so it is labeled “available to the dashboard,” not the default visible total.
7. **Non-RCT does not mean observational.** The exported records include quasi-experimental and before/after designs. The binary `is_rct` flag also disagrees with some design labels: 299 available rows have design label `RCT`, whereas only 282 rows are flagged randomized. The screen now says “flagged randomized” and “other / not flagged randomized”; independent design validation remains necessary.
8. **Reasons were incomplete and overinterpreted.** The old box showed only six reasons, and labeled 7,046 blank reasons “not a treatment trial / unspecified.” Blank reasons do not establish that rationale. The corrected display labels them “Unspecified” and includes an aggregated remainder so the reasons sum to the exclusion count.
9. **Search identification was overstated as a reconciled stage.** The generator used classification rows as identified records and subtracted relevance-positive rows to infer irrelevance. Unknown and parse-error decisions, duplicate handling, skipped article types, and changing data snapshots were not separately reconciled. The corrected page describes its limited scope and does not present this subtraction as an audited search flow.

## Sources and reproducibility

- Website: `app/birds-eye-reviews/long-covid/page.tsx` (server filter), `LongCovidDashboard.tsx` (publication preference and interactive filters), `data/birds_eye_reviews/long_covid/{trial_screening.csv,trial_extractions.jsonl,prisma.json}`.
- Upstream: `birds_eye_review_code/regen_long_covid_screening_data.py`, `projects/long_covid/data/{classifications.csv,relevance.csv,trial_screening.csv}`, `13_export_dashboard.py`.
- Legacy: `OLD_CODE_ARCHIVE/pull_long_covid_papers/{screen_for_trials.py,make_graphs.py,missing_pdfs_report.html}`. `make_graphs.py` contains a different, hard-coded 134-PDF-failure count for an older 676-clinical-trial cohort; it cannot be substituted for the 337-report log or today's snapshot.
- [PRISMA 2020 flow diagrams](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-flow-diagram) and [explanation and elaboration, item 16a](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8005925/) distinguish records, reports, studies, retrieval failures and eligibility exclusions.

The website computes its current counts with `computeScreeningCounts`; the dated failure list stays immutable. No upstream update files or existing exported research data were regenerated. To establish a complete current PRISMA flow, a future export needs a frozen, DOI-linked ledger of search/deduplication, screening decisions (including unknowns), retrieval attempts and successful full-text checks, extraction outcomes, report-to-study links and final eligibility. Do not infer reasons from differences in file lengths.

Validation: regression fixtures cover unresolved flags, extraction rows split by arm, previously excluded extractions, missing designs, unscreened exports, historical failures later extracted, and conservation of each accounting flow. The live-export checks independently reconcile all totals and confirm every historical DOI is unique.
